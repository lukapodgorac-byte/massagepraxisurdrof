Processing of personal data

You can generally visit this website without providing any personal details. Personal data (e.g. name, phone number, e-mail address) is only processed if you contact me of your own accord, for example for an appointment request.

Appointment requests

Appointment requests are handled via WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail. I use the data transmitted solely to process your enquiry and arrange an appointment. When using WhatsApp, the privacy terms of the respective provider also apply.

Server logs and hosting

When the website is accessed, the hosting provider may store technical data (e.g. IP address, date and time of access, browser type) in server log files. This data serves the secure and stable operation of the website.

Fonts

This website uses fonts from Google Fonts. When loading the fonts, a connection to Google servers may be established, during which your IP address may be transmitted.

Cookies and statistics

This website sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics services to evaluate your behaviour.

Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law you have in particular the right:

  • to request information about the data processed about you,
  • to have incorrect data corrected,
  • to request the deletion of your data, insofar as no retention obligation exists.

For information or concerns about data protection, you can reach me at claudia.mueller8@gmx.ch.
